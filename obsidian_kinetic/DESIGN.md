## Brand & Style

The design system projects high-caliber technical authority, architectural precision, and forward-leaning digital execution. Designed for enterprise clients, scaling startups, and high-growth brands seeking high-end digital engineering and interactive experiences, the visual tone avoids gimmicks in favor of deep atmospheric presence, razor-sharp typographic hierarchy, and luminous energy accents.

The aesthetic fuses **Refined Dark Mode Modernism** with **Atmospheric Glassmorphism**. Dark slate and obsidian voids create an immersive depth plane, punctuated by linear gradients of electric indigo, cyan, and teal. Translucent panels, crisp hair-thin boundaries, and controlled photonic glows provide a tactile sense of high-performance machinery and state-of-the-art software craftsmanship.

## Colors

The palette leverages an obsidian bedrock with controlled neon-range photon accents:

- **Base Surfaces & Backgrounds:** The canvas base rests on `#090D16` (Obsidian Void). Layered elevations stack through `#0F172A` (Midnight Slate) for mid-ground modules and `#1E293B` for elevated controls and cards.
- **Accents & Luminescence:** 
  - Primary `#6366F1` (Electric Indigo) drives key conversion hooks, structural highlights, and active indicators.
  - Secondary `#38BDF8` (Sky Cyan) and Tertiary `#06B6D4` (Teal Glow) provide vibrant directional gradients, telemetry points, and decorative focal glows.
- **Text & Content Hierarchy:** Text maintains high legibility against pitch backgrounds: `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50) for authoritative headlines, `#CBD5E1` (Slate 300) for readable body narrative, and `#64748B` (Slate 500) for structural metadata and inactive states.
- **Borders & Dividers:** Outlines use `#1E293B` modulated with alpha transparency (`rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.8)` down to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`) to maintain razor-thin glass delineation.

## Typography

The typography pairing contrasts the geometric, contemporary punch of **Plus Jakarta Sans** with the meticulous, neutral technical precision of **Inter**.

- **Display & Headlines:** Set in Plus Jakarta Sans with tight negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) and heavy weights (700 to 800) to communicate dynamic modernity, brand confidence, and engineering scale.
- **Body & Data:** Powered by Inter to maximize readability on high-density dashboards, technical capability breakdowns, and long-form proposal layouts.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Inter SemiBold with slight positive letter spacing provides high contrast and crisp legibility for status badges, tags, and small utility indicators.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a flexible 12-column fluid grid locked to a max width of 1440px on extra-large displays.

- **Breakpoints:**
  - Desktop: 1200px+ (12 columns, 24px gutters, 48px margins)
  - Tablet: 768px – 1199px (8 columns, 20px gutters, 32px margins)
  - Mobile: <767px (4 columns, 16px gutters, 20px margins)
- **Spatial Cadence:** Interior component relationships follow strict multiples of 4px and 8px. Macro sectioning employs substantial vertical breathing room (`space-2xl` and `space-3xl`) to let glowing atmospheric layers and high-contrast typography stand out cleanly.

## Elevation & Depth

Hierarchy is established via semi-translucent glass strata and radial atmospheric lighting:

1. **Layer 0 (Canvas Base):** Solid `#090D16` overlaid with subtle, large-radius radial gradients (`rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.08)` and `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.05)`) positioned dynamically behind major display groups.
2. **Layer 1 (Glass Container):** Background fill `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.65)` supported by a `12px` to `16px` backdrop-blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(14px)`). Surfaced with a 1px composite outline: `border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
3. **Layer 2 (Interactive Floating / Flyouts):** Background fill `rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.85)` with `20px` backdrop-blur, accompanied by a double-layer drop shadow:
   - Ambient: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`
   - Glow Accent: `0 0 24px -2px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.18)`
4. **Active/Hover Illumination:** Interactive cards gain a perimeter illumination using linear gradient border masks shifting from `rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.5)` to `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.2)`.

## Shapes

The shape philosophy balances modern softness with technical precision through a baseline `roundedness: 2` (0.5rem / 8px).

- **Standard Controls (Inputs, Buttons, Chips):** 8px border-radius (`0.5rem`) provides an ergonomic, contemporary silhouette.
- **Surface Cards & Modals:** Scaled to 16px (`rounded-lg`, 1rem) or 24px (`rounded-xl`, 1.5rem) to soften the perimeter of complex glass containers and technical dashboards.
- **Pill Varients:** Badges, live status indicators, and micro-pills leverage full pill rounding (`rounded-full`) to contrast against angular grid blocks.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Linear gradient fill `linear-gradient(135deg, #6366F1 0%, #38BDF8 100%)`, text `#FFFFFF`, label-md font weight. Hover state initiates an intensity shift and an external aura (`box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary (Glass):** Background `rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.6)`, 1px border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, text `#F8FAFC`. On hover, the border brightens to `#38BDF8` with a background transition to `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.1)`.
- **Ghost:** Transparent background, text `#94A3B8`, hovering to `#F8FAFC` with subtle background glow `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`.

### Cards & Panels
- **Default Card:** Surface `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.7)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`. Outlined by `1px solid rgba(51, 65, 85, 0.6)`. Card inner content padded with `space-lg` to `space-xl`.
- **Feature/Metric Card:** Incorporates top-edge linear highlight lines (`1px height`, gradient fading from `transparent` to `#38BDF8` to `transparent`).

### Input Fields
- Dark input fields filled with `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.85)` framed by `1px solid #1E293B`.
- Placeholder text in `#64748B`.
- Focus state drops standard blue outlines in favor of a crisp 1px ring in `#6366F1` and a soft ambient glow `0 0 0 3px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.25)`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status/Tech Chips:** `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.9)` with 1px border `rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.3)`. Text color `#38BDF8`, sized to `label-sm`.
- **Live State Indicator:** Circular 6px dot with continuous radial cyan pulse animation (`#06B6D4`).

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Square (checkbox, 4px radius) or circular (radio) base built on `#0F172A` with `#334155` border.
- Selected state fills with `#6366F1`, crisp white SVG glyph check, and a 4px soft indigo aura.

### Specialized Agency Modules
- **Project Showcase Tile:** Glassmorphic container with 16:9 imagery framed under an inner inset border shadow (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`), featuring hover zoom and title slide-up.
- **Code/Architecture Snippet Box:** Pitch slate surface (`#070B12`), monospaced code layout, header displaying status dot indicators (cyan, indigo, slate), and clean copy-to-clipboard action triggers.
